
# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard – Power BI Project

## 📌 Overview
This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Performance Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI** by **Saksham Kajla**.  
The dashboard provides key insights into global sales performance, profits, shipping costs, product categories, and market segmentation across countries and regions.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 🧾 Page 1: Sales Performance Overview
**Highlights**
- **KPIs:**  
  - 💰 Total Sales – 12.64M  
  - 💹 Total Profit – 1.47M  
  - 📦 Total Quantity Sold – 178K  
  - 🚚 Total Shipping Cost – 1.35M  

**Visual Insights**
- **Sales by Country:** United States leads with 42.46% of total sales.  
- **Sales by State:** Top-performing states include England and California.  
- **Sales by Category:** Technology dominates, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.  
- **Sales by Market:** APAC, EU, and US contribute major sales volume.  
- **Sales by Ship Mode:** Standard Class covers over 60% of all shipments.

---

### 🌍 Page 2: Global Distribution & Regional Insights
**Highlights**
- **Total Count:**  
  - 🌎 147 Countries  
  - 🗺️ 1094 States  
  - 🏙️ 3636 Cities  

**Visual Insights**
- **Sum of Sales by Country & State:** Highlights performance distribution across global regions.  
- **Sum of Sales by Region:** Comparison of regional revenue, with North America and Europe leading.  
- **Sum of Discount by Sub-Category:** Top discounts in Binders, Chairs, and Phones.  
- **Map Visualization:** Displays customer and order density across the world.

---

## 🧩 Data Model
Key data fields used:
- **Dimensions:** Country, State, City, Category, Sub-Category, Market, Ship Mode  
- **Measures:** Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount, Shipping Cost  
- **Additional Fields:** Customer ID, Order ID, Product ID, Segment, Region

This dashboard delivers a clear, visual, and data-driven understanding of e-commerce operations — from sales trends and profitability to regional performance and shipping analysis.
